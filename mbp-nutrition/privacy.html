Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2026. We respect your privacy just as seriously as we treat ingredient transparency.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse MBP Nutrition, submit a Quick Order or Product Enquiry, subscribe to our newsletter, or leave a verified product review, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact & Order Details: Full name, email address, phone number, shipping city/address, and selected product flavor or pack size.
  • Enquiry & Nutrition Preferences: Messages submitted via our Contact Us form (Product Query, Bulk Order, or Nutrition Advice) and customer reviews submitted on product pages.
  • Device & Usage Telemetry: Browser type, device viewport, referring URLs, pages viewed, and search queries to improve site usability and catalog relevance.

2. How We Use Your Information

MBP Nutrition uses collected information strictly to operate and improve our e-commerce and customer nutrition services:

  • To process quick orders, bulk enquiries, shipping notifications, and support requests.
  • To provide requested sports nutrition guidance and send your first-order welcome discount (code MBP10) when you opt in to our newsletter.
  • To monitor website performance, prevent fraudulent submissions, and comply with applicable Indian e-commerce and FSSAI record-keeping regulations.

3. Cookies, Analytics & Advertising Tracking

We use essential browser storage and cookies to keep our website fast, responsive, and personalized:

  • Essential & Functional Storage: Preserves your submitted product reviews, search context, and newsletter opt-in state locally in your browser.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand aggregate traffic patterns (such as popular supplement categories and recipe guides) so we can optimize navigation and page load speeds.
  • Ad Tracking & Attribution Cookies: Used only to measure campaign effectiveness (for example, whether an athlete discovered our Whey Protein Isolate via a fitness newsletter or social campaign) and avoid showing repetitive promotions.

You can manage, block, or clear cookies at any time through your browser privacy settings. Disabling non-essential cookies will not prevent you from browsing products, reading recipes, or submitting enquiries.

4. Customer Information Security

We protect customer information using HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, strict role-based internal access controls, and minimal data retention practices. MBP Nutrition does not sell, rent, or trade your personal contact information to third-party data brokers. Trusted logistics and payment partners receive only the minimum details necessary to fulfill your order or enquiry.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You may request to access, correct, export, or delete any personal information we hold about you, or opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting our Data Protection Officer.

6. Contact Details for Privacy Inquiries

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, cookie preferences, or your personal data, please contact us:

  • Privacy Email: privacy@mbpnutrition.com
  • General Support: support@mbpnutrition.com
  • Postal Address: Data Protection Desk, MBP Nutrition Wellness Labs Pvt. Ltd., Level 3, 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038, India
